<p>This page is served by busybox httpd on 127.0.0.1:80 inside a measured
SEV-SNP guest. It is bound to loopback and to nothing else, so the ethernet
segment between the two guests cannot reach it: the only route to it is this
guest's own exit, at the far end of a tunnel a peer was admitted on.</p>

written by /sbin/init out of the sandbox_id on the config device, which is what
tells one guest from the other when both booted the same image.</p>
