Still curl, and not `attest-tool provision fetch`: that subcommand is the AMD chain tool and
nothing else. Its only inputs are `-report REPORT.bin -out DIR`, it parses that file as an
SEV-SNP attestation report and calls provision.Fetch to pull the VCEK chain for the platform

TCB info, a QE identity or a CRL. That is ticket 22's deviation and it is carried forward
unchanged here: ADR-0005 and ADR-0007 require the Intel documents to be provisioned ahead of
use, not that one binary do the provisioning, and what is checked is not the fetch but the
result -- `attest-tool verify -vendor intel-tdx` against this directory, plus the ticket 21
byte-level verdict replay. Both are recorded in
