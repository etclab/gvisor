    attest/verify/snp.go all say a stale chain surfaces at the peer as MALFORMED
    EVIDENCE, because the library compares the reported TCB against the one the
    chain was issued for. On real silicon it does not. The VCEK is derived from
    the chip secret AND the TCB, so a chain for microcode 71 endorses a different

    would have said malformed. Still fail-closed, still no fetch; but an operator
    told to look for "malformed evidence" will not find it, and this refusal's
    detail does not name ADR-0005. The local check in provision.CheckFor (case 6
