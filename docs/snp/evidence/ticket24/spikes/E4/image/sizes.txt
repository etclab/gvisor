The whole cost is in the initrd, and it is smaller than the binary that caused
it: gzip -9 over the cpio turns 108668311 bytes of runsc into 76104756 bytes
of image, 70.0% of the binary. There is no verity root here to hide it in --
on SEV-SNP runsc went into the squashfs and the initrd grew by 849 bytes
(docs/snp/evidence/ticket24/spikes/E2/sizes.txt); here the initramfs IS the root,
grub measures it whole, and so every one of those bytes is inside RTMR2.

The initrd's entry count went 49 -> 52: /usr/bin/runsc, dir /workload, and the
unshare applet symlink.
